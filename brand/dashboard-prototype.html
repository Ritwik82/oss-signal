IV METHOD

Six signals. One honest score.

No black boxes. Each score is a weighted sum of six transparent signals — open any card, hover a dot, and see exactly what moved.

  1. 01

    Recency

    How fresh the last release and commit are. Stale code is the first warning.

    WEIGHT 0.20
  2. 02

    Momentum

    Commit velocity trended over quarters — not spikes, not one-off pushes.

    WEIGHT 0.20
  3. 03

    Issue health

    Open versus resolved issues and triage cadence. Slipping triage shows here first.

    WEIGHT 0.15
  4. 04

    Contributors

    Active committers on recent releases — not lifetime contributor totals.

    WEIGHT 0.15
  5. 05

    License

    The project is verifiably free and open — license parsed and checked.

    WEIGHT 0.15
  6. 06

    Abandonment risk

    Composite decay flag. Stale apps don't get to ride on momentum.

    WEIGHT 0.15
